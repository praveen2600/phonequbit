Magnet test (compass)

Does a magnet near the phone change the compass heading that the browser gives? This page checks it.

  1. Use Safari (on Android: Chrome). Lay the phone flat on a hard table. Tap Start.
  2. Keep the phone still. Wait until the heading is steady, then tap Set heading to zero.
  3. Slide the earbud case along the table, slowly, toward the top edge of the phone. Do not put it on the phone. Tap Magnet near.
  4. Take it away. Tap Magnet away.
  5. Repeat 3 times, also from the sides. Then tap Share log or Copy log.
