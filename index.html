    Try this

    Measure along x (jab)

    Tap Reset. Twist one quarter turn clockwise: the state is now |+x⟩. Jab the phone left–right (a quick straight push and stop). The result is +x every time. Now jab up and down: you get 0 or 1 at random.

    The axis matters, not the direction

    A jab up and a jab down both measure along z. A jab left and a jab right both measure along x.

    Measuring along x changes the state

    Tap Reset, so the state is |0⟩. Jab left–right: you get +x or −x at random. Now tap to measure along z: 0 or 1, at random again, although the state was |0⟩ before.

    Phase you cannot see

    Tap Reset. Twist one quarter turn and keep the phone in landscape. Now flick it: turn it a little about a vertical line through its middle, like a door, and straight back. The colour stays purple and the chance of 1 stays 50%. Only the phase changed (see More → State).

    Phase that changes the result

    Tap Reset. Twist one quarter turn. Flick twice (phase 180°). Twist one more quarter turn in the same direction and measure. The result is always 0. Without the flicks it is always 1.

    Can you see the phase?

    Tap Reset. Twist one quarter turn clockwise and flick once (phase 90°). Tap to measure, then Repeat, ten times: about half are 1, as without the flick. Now jab toward–away, with Repeat before each jab: the same y result every time. Jab left–right: random. The phase is invisible along z and decides the result along y.

    Hadamard gate

    Flick twice, then twist one quarter turn clockwise. This is the Hadamard gate H. From |0⟩ it gives |+⟩, with the arrow along +x. Do it twice and you are back at |0⟩. From |1⟩ it gives |−⟩, with the arrow along −x. (An anticlockwise twist gives a different gate: from |0⟩ it gives |−⟩.)

    Superposition

    Tap Reset. Twist one quarter turn and tap Pause. Tap to measure; then Repeat and measure, until the screen shows Measurement 20. About half the results are 1.

    Collapse

    Measure. Then measure again without twisting. You get the same result every time.

    Interference

    Tap Reset. Twist one quarter turn. Tap Pause, bring the phone upright, and tap Go on. Twist one more quarter turn in the same direction. Measure: the result is always 1. Now measure after the first quarter turn too. The final result becomes random again.

    Field pulse

    In Activities, start “Field pulse”. Cover the camera just long enough for a 90° turn and measure; then Repeat and measure, 20 times in all. About half the results are 1. Tap “Reset to |0⟩” and try a 180° pulse: every result is 1.

    Uneven chances

    Choose “Smooth angle” in Settings. Reset and twist to 60°. Measure 20 times, tapping Repeat before each measurement. About 1 in 4 results is 1.

    Try this

    The 1922 experiment

    Tap M1 in the strip and choose “Off”. Atoms now come straight from the oven, with random spins. Send atoms at any angle. You always get about half up and half down, in two separate spots.

    Upright

    Hold the phone upright, so φ = 0°. Send atoms. They all go up.

    Sideways

    Twist to φ = 90° and tap Pause. Send 20 atoms. About half go up.

    Upside down

    Turn the phone to φ = 180°. All atoms go down. Notice that “down” on the phone now points up in the room.

    Three in four

    Find the angle where about 3 of 4 atoms go up. Compare it with cos²(φ/2) = 0.75 at φ = 60°.

    Prepare spin along x

    Tap M1 and choose “Up along x”. Hold the phone upright, so magnet 2 points along z: about half go up. Now twist to 90°, along x: all go up.

    Three magnets: z, then x, then z

    Tap “+ Magnet 3”. Twist the phone to 90° and tap “Set magnet 2”. Hold the phone upright and send atoms. About half go down, although magnet 1 removed every down atom. Now tap M2, set it to 0°, and try again. All atoms go up.
